# Big Billion Days Regional Performance Dashboard

## 1. Dashboard Objective

The dashboard will help users understand regional sales performance during the Big Billion Days sale. It will highlight revenue, completed orders, product performance, and monthly trends.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total completed orders: 63
- Total revenue: 346,200
- Total units sold: 106
- Average order value: Total revenue divided by completed orders

## 3. Recommended Visualizations

### Revenue by Region
Use a bar chart to compare total completed-order revenue across regions.

### Monthly Revenue Trend
Use a line chart to compare revenue across September and October 2026.

### Orders by Status
Use a bar chart to compare completed, pending, and cancelled orders.

### Product Revenue Contribution
Use a bar chart to compare revenue generated by each product.

### Sales Representative Performance
Use a table to compare each representative's monthly revenue with their target.

## 4. Filters

The dashboard should include filters for region, order month, product, and order status.

## 5. Data Source

Use `data/orders_enriched.csv` for the enriched order-level dataset. Use completed orders when calculating completed-order revenue and KPIs.

## 6. Validation

Before publishing the dashboard, compare its totals with the SQL query results to confirm that the calculations are consistent.

## 7. Conclusion

This dashboard will provide a clear view of regional performance, product contribution, and monthly sales trends to support data-driven decisions.
